## What an AI Meeting Assistant Actually Does (and Why "Transcription" Is the Wrong Thing to Compare)

An AI meeting assistant joins your calls, records them, and produces a transcript, a summary, and — in the tools worth paying for — extracted action items. In 2025 and 2026, the big three all transcribe accurately. That's table stakes. The real differences live downstream:

- **Action-item extraction** — does it pull clean, owner-attributed tasks out of messy conversation, or does it dump 40 bullets you still have to triage?
- **PM-tool sync** — can it push those tasks straight into Todoist, Notion, Linear, or Salesforce without you copy-pasting?
- **Follow-up enforcement** — does it re-surface stale action items, nag the assignee, and cut the "did you get my note?" emails?
- **Meeting-type coverage** — a sales call demands CRM logging and next-step tracking; a brainstorm needs idea clustering; an interview needs role-tagged transcript tabs.

The tools that only transcribe well have become commodity. The tools that close the loop from "said" to "done" are the ones worth a monthly subscription — and that's the gap most 2026 comparisons miss entirely.

## Step-by-Step: Auto-Create Todoist and Notion Tasks From Every Meeting

This is the workflow that turns a meeting recorder into a follow-up machine. Do this once and every future meeting auto-creates tracked tasks — here's how to set it up, tested across all three tools.

1. **Connect your calendar and task apps first.** In Fireflies (or Fathom), go to Integrations and authorize your calendar, then connect Todoist or Notion. Grant calendar read so the assistant can join automatically by default — you shouldn't have to schedule it per-call.

2. **Turn on "automatic join."** For Fireflies, toggle the calendar integration to automatically add the assistant to every meeting. In Fathom, set your recording preference to "always join." This is the step people skip — a recorder you have to remember to start isn't a recorder.

3. **Enable action-item extraction.** In Fireflies, open Settings → AI & Workflows and switch on "Auto-detect action items." In Fathom, this is on by default in the summary settings. Make sure "assign owner automatically" is enabled so tasks carry a name, not just a verb.

4. **Set the push-to-task mapping.** Configure which extracted items go where. For Notion, create a "Meeting Action Items" database and map the "Assignee," "Due date," and "Status" fields to AI-generated values. For Todoist, pick the project (e.g., "Work") so new tasks land in the right list.

5. **Schedule the push.** In Fireflows (Fireflies' automation), create a workflow: when a meeting ends → create task → set due date to "tomorrow" → assign to the mentioned owner. Fathom does this natively with its "Push to Notion" one-click.

6. **Test with a fake call.** Have a colleague hold a 10-minute call where you deliberately say "action item: send the pricing doc to Alex." Check that the task lands in Notion/Todoist with the right owner and due date. Fix any mapping before it's live.

7. **Set the follow-up cadence.** For tasks that go stale, configure Fireflies to email the assignee a reminder after 48 hours. Fathom relies on your PM tool's own reminders, so enable those in Notion or Todoist.

Done — from now on, every meeting feeds your task manager automatically. The tool stops being a passive transcript and becomes the thing that makes sure Alex actually gets the pricing doc.

## FAQ

**Which AI meeting assistant is most accurate at transcription?**
All three exceed ~95% accuracy in 2026. Accuracy is a solved problem. The deciding factor should be action-item extraction and task sync, not word-for-word accuracy, because that's where the tools genuinely diverge.

**Can I push meeting action items straight into Notion or Todoist?**
Yes. Fireflies and Fathom both push extracted tasks to Notion and Todoist automatically. Fireflies is stronger for multi-step automation and reminders; Fathom is faster for a simple one-click push.

**Which is best for a sales team using Salesforce?**
Fathom is the crowd favorite for sales flows and syncs cleanly to Salesforce, but Fireflies matches it and adds stronger follow-up reminder automation. Pick Fathom if you want simplicity, Fireflies if you want aggressive task enforcement.

**Do these tools work with Teams and Google Meet?**
Yes. All three support Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Read.ai additionally covers Webex and is the safest pick for older enterprise conferencing stacks.

**Is the free plan actually useful?**
Yes. Fireflies gives you unlimited transcription on a 3-meetings-per-day cap, Fathom gives unlimited transcription free, and Read.ai offers a credit-based free tier. All are genuinely enough to evaluate the workflow before paying.
